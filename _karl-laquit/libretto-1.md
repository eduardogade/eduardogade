# Nothing Twice

### Two poetic adaptations for Karl Laquit

*Italian · German · A future piece for piano, cello, and voice*

---

## A note to Karl

Dear Karl,

You are a very special person. Not just because of the extraordinary things you can do with your voice, but because of the person you are when the music stops. Some people leave a trace in our lives that time doesn't quite erase. You are one of those people for me.

Sometimes I think I made one of the biggest mistakes of my life. **Twice.** Which is almost an achievement, considering that the poem at the heart of this little project is called *Nothing Twice*. Apparently, even when life refuses to repeat itself, I can still manage to make the same kind of mistake more than once.

So here's my rather questionable arithmetic. In my book of regrets, let's assign those two mistakes **−2**. I'd like to offer you **+1**: something new, made especially for you. The balance, naturally, is still **−1**—one regret left on the books. I know a piece of music can't simply cancel out the past. But I hope it can make something beautiful in the present.

Below you'll find two poetic versions of the same poem: **Italian** and **German**. Pick whichever speaks to you most. I'll turn it into a piece for **piano, cello, and voice**, written with *your* voice in mind. Not just its remarkable range, but everything you can express with it.

And if you hate it, I'll do it again.

After all, the poem says nothing happens twice. It wouldn't be the first time I've broken a rule anyway. :-)

With affection,  
**Eduardo**

## I. Italiano

### *Due volte, mai*

*Adattamento poetico in italiano*

Nulla può accadere due volte, mai;  
e questo è il triste dato:  
arriviamo improvvisando, sai,  
e partiamo senza aver provato.

Fossi anche il più sciocco tra la gente,  
il più gran somaro che ci sia,  
d'estate non ripeti proprio niente:  
questo corso si fa una volta e via.

Nessun giorno è la copia di ieri,  
né due notti hanno egual tenerezza:  
non insegnano gli stessi piaceri  
con gli stessi baci e la stessa carezza.

Un giorno, forse, una distratta voce  
pronuncia il tuo nome per errore:  
mi pare che una rosa voli veloce  
nella stanza, tutta profumo e colore.

Il giorno dopo, anche se sei con me,  
fisso l'orologio per ore:  
una rosa? Una rosa? Che cos'è?  
È una pietra oppure un fiore?

Perché il giorno che fugge trattiamo  
con tanto dolore e paura?  
Domani l'oggi è già perso, lo sappiamo:  
non restare è la sua natura.

Con baci e sorrisi preferiamo  
cercare intesa sotto la nostra stella;  
pur diversi — e in ciò concordiamo —  
come due gocce d'acqua, l'una all'altra gemella.

## II. Deutsch

### *Nichts geschieht zweimal*

*Deutsche Nachdichtung*

Nichts auf der Welt geschieht zweimal,  
das ist die traurige Kunde:  
Wir kommen ungeübt und ohne Wahl  
und gehn noch vor der Übungsstunde.

Und wärst du der Dümmste auf Erden,  
der größte Narr weit und breit:  
Im Sommer kann nichts nachgeholt werden;  
den Kurs gibt's einmal nur, für alle Zeit.

Kein Tag ist wie der Tag zuvor,  
zwei Nächte lehren nie dasselbe Glück;  
kein Kuss kommt je genauso vor,  
und keiner kehrt auf gleiche Art zurück.

Vielleicht fällt, ganz nebenbei,  
dein Name, unbedacht;  
mir fliegt 'ne Rose dann herbei,  
ins Zimmer – Duft und Farbenpracht.

Am nächsten Tag, du bist bei mir,  
doch starr ich ständig auf die Uhr.  
'ne Rose? 'ne Rose? Was ist das hier?  
'ne Blume? Ein Stein? Was nur?

Warum beschweren wir den Tag  
mit Trauer und mit Bangen?  
Er bleibt nicht, weil er's nicht vermag:  
Schon morgen ist heut vergangen.

Mit Lächeln, Küssen suchen wir  
den Einklang unterm Sternenzelt;  
verschieden sind wir – einig hier –,  
wie zwei Wassertropfen auf der Welt.

### About the text

These Italian and German versions are **poetic adaptations**, not official translations, of *Nic dwa razy* (*Nothing Twice*) by **Wisława Szymborska**. They were developed from an English rendition of the poem, with an emphasis on retaining its imagery and alternating rhyme scheme. They have not yet been adjusted to a finalized musical meter or vocal setting.
